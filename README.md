# 🎓 AspireLink

AspireLink is an AI-powered platform that bridges the gap between students, mentors, and industry professionals by offering a personalized career development ecosystem.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔐 Role-Based Access
- Distinct dashboards and functionalities for **Students**, **Mentors**, and **Admins**.

### 🧠 Personalized Support
- One-on-one mentorship based on skillset.
- Open forums for discussions between students and industry professionals.

### 📄 Resume & ATS Tools
- Resume analyzer for feedback on structure, content, and skills.
- **ATS-Optimized Resume Builder** to increase job application success.

### 💼 Internship & Job Matching
- Skill-based internship recommendations.
- Mentor & job matching engine with:
  - Match Success Rate charts.
  - Industry demand vs. available job graphs.

### 📊 Dashboards & Analytics
- Dashboards tailored for students, mentors, and admins.
- **Career Interest Analysis** (AI-based career suggestions).
  - Bar/Pie charts showing interest across domains (Tech, Medicine, Business, etc.)
- **Skill Gap Analysis** using:
  - Radar charts to compare student vs. industry skills.
  - Line charts to show improvement over time.

### 📚 Learning & Development
- Curated **courses** based on student interests and goals.
- Skill-based **quiz/test** system.
- Personalized Learning Plans (PLPs) for guided development.

### 📈 Career Progress Tracker
- Goal setting & progress monitoring.
- Skill assessment reports.
- Visual roadmaps for learning and development.

### 🤝 Communication & Support
- Video conferencing & chat system for:
  - Student-mentor collaboration.
  - Mock interviews with industry professionals.
- Multilingual chatbot for real-time support.

### 🎓 Additional Offerings
- Scholarship recommendations.
- Webinars and career guidance talks.
